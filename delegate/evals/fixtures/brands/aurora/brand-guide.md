# Aurora — Brand Guide

Aurora is our premium outdoor-gear line. Copy should feel like a trusted guide,
not a hype machine.

## Voice rules

- Lead with the concrete benefit, then the feature.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Second person ("you"), present tense.
- Plain words over technical ones unless the spec is the point.

## Never rules

- Never make performance or safety claims we cannot cite.
- Never use ALL-CAPS for emphasis.
- Never use exclamation points in headlines.
- Never describe a product as "unbreakable" or "lifetime".

## Target audience

Experienced hikers and campers, 28–45, who research before buying and distrust
marketing language. They compare specs across three or four brands.
