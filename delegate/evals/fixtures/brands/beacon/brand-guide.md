# Beacon — Brand Guide

Beacon is our entry-level everyday line. Copy should feel warm, direct, and
unintimidating to someone buying their first set.

## Voice rules

- Friendly and encouraging, never condescending.
- Explain the jargon the first time it appears.
- Contractions are fine ("you'll", "it's").
- Keep paragraphs to two sentences on mobile-first surfaces.

## Never rules

- Never make performance or safety claims we cannot cite.
- Never use ALL-CAPS for emphasis.
- Never imply the customer is doing it wrong.
- Never stack more than two adjectives on a product name.

## Target audience

First-time buyers, 22–35, price-sensitive, shopping mostly on phones. They want
reassurance that they picked something reasonable.
