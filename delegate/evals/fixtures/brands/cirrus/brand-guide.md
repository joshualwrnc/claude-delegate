# Cirrus — Brand Guide

Cirrus is our technical/lightweight line. Copy should be precise and
spec-forward; the audience reads the numbers first.

## Voice rules

- Numbers before adjectives. Cite weight, volume, and materials early.
- Neutral, engineering-report tone.
- Define a term once, then use it consistently.
- No storytelling openers — get to the spec.

## Never rules

- Never make performance or safety claims we cannot cite.
- Never use ALL-CAPS for emphasis.
- Never round a spec in the customer's favor.
- Never compare directly to a named competitor.

## Target audience

Weight-conscious thru-hikers and alpinists, 25–50, who will notice a 20-gram
discrepancy and post about it.
